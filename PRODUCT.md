# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

B2B decision-makers (project directors, procurement heads, infrastructure managers at utilities, mining companies, and government entities) and technical evaluators (engineers reviewing capabilities, certifications, and past project records). Both visit the same pages in the same session: directors skim for credibility signals, engineers drill for technical depth. Neither tolerates filler.

## Product Purpose

Institutional website for Hidromont Chile S.A. — a hydraulic engineering contractor with 40+ years in penstock fabrication, gates, valves, turbines, trash racks, and electromechanical assemblies. The site converts B2B prospects: move them from awareness to contact. Success looks like a procurement director feeling confident enough to initiate a project conversation after 90 seconds on any page.

## Brand Personality

Monumental, precise, proven. Not conservative — experienced. The structures Hidromont builds are massive; the communication should feel like it.

## Anti-references

- Generic B2B industrial split hero: text-left, image-right, uppercase tracked eyebrow, navy background, stock photo grid. The pattern every scaffold generates.
- Hero-metric blocks: "40+ years / 100+ projects / 3 countries" in big-number boxes. SaaS cliché applied to infrastructure.
- Mining sector visual monotony: hardhats in PPE, corporate-blue safety aesthetic, clip-art icons.

## Design Principles

1. **Infrastructure scale** — compositions feel as large as the structures Hidromont builds. Nothing cramped or contained.
2. **Precision over decoration** — every element earns its place. Real project photography outweighs icons and patterns.
3. **The project is the hero** — photography and engineering documentation over illustration.
4. **Dual-register legibility** — directors scan the top level, engineers drill deeper. Both paths work without noise between them.
5. **Industrial restraint** — the design system (dark navy, primary blue, zero border-radius) is a constraint, not a decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Reduced motion handled via `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` in motion.css. All images need descriptive, CMS-editable alt text. Spanish only (Chilean B2B market, no i18n needed).
